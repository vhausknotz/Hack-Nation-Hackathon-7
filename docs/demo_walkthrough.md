## Maria's research-coordination journey

Start with **SNAP25**. The name locates a diagnosis, but does not by itself tell a family who can help.

1. **You are here:** a plain description and position on the map. Explain that map proximity is computed from recorded symptoms and gene biology; it is a lead, not a finding. The map is not geography.
2. **Find your people:** the SNAP25 Foundation, with its scope, quoted source and single-AI-review label. Missing listings elsewhere mean our search is incomplete.
3. **You're not alone:** open the STXBP1 connection and “Why connected?”. The source-backed features explain why it is a research lead. Same-gene diagnoses are deliberately separate; gene similarity alone is insufficient.
4. **What already exists:** explore the registry listing and research programs, keeping their reported status and restrictions. “Recruiting” is what a record said, not a promise of current enrollment or eligibility.
5. **Prepare your questions:** “Research already connecting communities” identifies the same reviewed registry record, NCT01238250, on SNAP25 and STXBP1. Open both diagnoses' evidence. This supports asking the record's team about the current protocol and reusable questionnaires/data definitions, not assuming the cohorts can be combined. Any separately listed community is a contact to investigate; its involvement in that record is not established by the atlas.
6. Use **Copy question + sources** for a focused draft about that shared record. It keeps both diagnoses' restrictions, review labels, source dates and evidence IDs. Nothing is sent. Open **Question brief** for the full context. Copy or print the diagnosis, existing assets, exact sources, restrictions, shared-research question and recorded review dates. This is something Maria can take to her group and study team before paying to rebuild an asset.

Then open **STXBP1**. In step 5, the shared STXBP1/SYNGAP1 natural-history record **NCT06555965** is a more focused example. The source includes both genes and eligibility exclusions. The concrete question is whether its protocol or data definitions can inform the group's work, subject to scientific fit, consent and permissions. The listing does not show treatment benefit.

Step 6 now shows **How this part of the atlas was checked**. Expand the natural-history listing: source archived → claim submitted → deterministic source checks → actual Sol review and qualification. These are recorded events, not a simulated live agent or a fresh availability check. All current model reviews come from one family.

## The atlas correcting its own evidence

This happened for real; no replay writes or paid calls are needed for a presentation.

- A contributor used authenticated hosted MCP to fetch the official NCT06555965 record and propose full eligibility evidence for STXBP1 and SYNGAP1.
- The kernel rejected the first two proposals because the purpose quotes exceeded its size limit. Neither reached paid review.
- Corrected proposals retained the complete eligibility restrictions and passed schema, ID, signature, archived-quote and source-hash checks.
- A pinned local operator plan allowed only two Sol review attempts. Both supported the listings; STXBP1's review explicitly kept its qualification.
- One bounded invocation exported data, built the app, checked desktop/phone behavior, published the MCP snapshot, deployed the website and checked the live result.
- An unchanged restart made no extra model calls and skipped publication. The lifetime two-attempt allowance remains exhausted.

Receipt: [mcp-shared-study-result.json](../data/campaigns/mcp-shared-study-result.json). Commands and failure behavior: [CYCLE.md](../atlas_mcp/CYCLE.md). Source and review are distinct: mechanical acceptance does not establish biological truth. The preserved first rejection was a quote-size violation, not detection of a false biomedical conclusion.

## Demonstrate the limits too

- **Hutchinson-Gilford progeria:** search “progeria”; it ranks before broad groups. Archived organization sources stay visibly historical. No atlas study listing does not mean no progeria trial exists.
- **ARF3-related neurodevelopmental disorder:** sparse symptoms/listings produce explicit gaps, not invented recommendations. It has no listing-check timeline to fabricate.
- **STXBP2 versus STXBP1:** similar names need not imply similar disease biology.

## What is built versus still ahead

Built: explainable breadth map; useful family/group journey; reviewed listings; source-preserving brief; authenticated MCP contributions; bounded, resumable local review-to-publication cycle; visible listing provenance.

Ahead: genuinely independent review, independently reviewed partnership proposals, broader named professional collaborators, systematic freshness and continuous funded research operations. MCP intake is hosted; the ledger worker runs on this computer. New contributors require enrollment and review funding. The background Luna screening produces candidates, not published knowledge.

Do not claim a measured 10× acceleration, a treatment recommendation, universal coverage, continuous autonomous research, or independent verification. The demonstrated benefit is finding existing work and preparing a precise, sourced question about what might be reused.
